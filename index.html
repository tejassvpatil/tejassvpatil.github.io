04 / About

A computer scientist interested in the messy middle between models and people.

My research asks how AI systems can become useful, trustworthy parts of everyday care—not just high-performing models in isolation.

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at UC Davis, advised by Dr. Alyssa Weakley and Dr. Chen-Nee Chuah. At UC Davis Health, I work across machine learning, human-computer interaction, sensing, and production software to support cognitive health and remote caregiving.

Before graduate school, I was a software engineer building commercial banking platforms. That background still shapes how I work: I care about evaluation, but I also care about privacy boundaries, system responsiveness, failure modes, and whether a tool fits into a person’s actual day.
