Day 1 — Friday, June 12

Late-Start Redwoods

🌲 Armstrong / coast:
Cooler than Davis • Bring layers
📍 Davis → Armstrong Redwoods → optional Bodega Bay → Davis
🕘 Leave Davis 12:40 PM • Return around 9:00–10:00 PM
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~210–240 miles round trip
🗺️ Open Redwoods route 🌲 Armstrong park info

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 12:40 PMLeave Davis. Eat before leaving or carry lunch/snacks; this is now an afternoon plan.
  • 3:00 PMArrive Armstrong Redwoods. Prioritize the main grove, restroom, and an easy loop.
  • 3:15–4:45 PMPioneer Nature Trail / main grove. See Colonel Armstrong Tree and Parson Jones Tree without rushing.
  • 5:00 PMDecision point. If everyone has energy, continue to Bodega Bay; if not, dinner in Guerneville/Santa Rosa and return.
  • 6:00–7:00 PMOptional Bodega Bay coast. Quick coastal view, coffee/snack, photos, and layers.
  • 7:15 PMStart return to Davis. Keep this simple so Tahoe tomorrow still feels good.
  • 9–10 PMArrive Davis. Pack Tahoe layers and snacks before sleeping.

Highlights

  • Armstrong Redwoods main grove
  • Pioneer Nature Trail
  • Colonel Armstrong Tree
  • Parson Jones Tree
  • Optional Bodega Bay sunset/coast

Food

Eat before leaving Davis • coffee/snack near Guerneville • dinner in Santa Rosa or on the return
⭐ Point Reyes removed because 12:40 PM is too late for a relaxed version

Day 2 — Saturday, June 13

Lake Tahoe Day Trip

🏔️ South Lake Tahoe:
Sunny mountain day • Cool evening
📍 Davis → Emerald Bay → South Lake Tahoe → Heavenly Village → Davis
🕘 Leave 7:00 AM • Return around 8:00–9:00 PM
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~220 miles round trip
🗺️ Open Tahoe route 🏞️ Emerald Bay info

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 7:00 AMLeave Davis. Bring water, layers, and patience for Saturday lake traffic.
  • 8:15 AMPlacerville breakfast stop. Coffee, restroom, and snacks.
  • 10:30 AMEmerald Bay overlook. Main photo stop; parking can be tight.
  • 12:00 PMSouth Lake Tahoe lunch. Keep it casual near the lake or Heavenly Village.
  • 1:30 PMLakeside walk / beach time. Choose one low-effort lake stop rather than chasing too many points.
  • 3:30 PMHeavenly Village. Flexible final activity, coffee, shops, or scenic stroll.
  • 5:30 PMStart return drive. Dinner on the way back if needed.
  • 8–9 PMArrive Davis. Keep Sunday morning easy before Yosemite.

Highlights

  • Emerald Bay overlook
  • South Lake Tahoe lunch
  • Lakeside walk or beach stop
  • Heavenly Village
  • Scenic mountain drive

Food

Breakfast stop in Placerville • lunch by the lake • dinner on return
⭐ Keep Tahoe as a clean round trip before the Yosemite overnight

Day 3 — Sunday, June 14

Yosemite Arrival + Overnight

⛰️ Yosemite evening:
Late arrival • Stay overnight
📍 Davis → Oakdale / Mariposa → Yosemite area lodging
🕘 Leave 1:00 PM onward • Arrive Yosemite area around evening
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~180–220 miles depending on lodging
🗺️ Open Yosemite arrival route ⚠️ Yosemite conditions

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • Before 1 PMPack overnight bags. Keep snacks, water, chargers, and layers accessible.
  • 1:00 PMLeave Davis. This is mainly a positioning day, not a full Yosemite sightseeing day.
  • 3:00 PMOakdale / Mariposa stop. Gas, restroom, coffee, and dinner supplies if lodging is remote.
  • 5:30–6:30 PMArrive Yosemite area. Check in first if lodging is outside/near the park.
  • EveningLight scenic stop only. Tunnel View or a short valley stroll if time, parking, and energy line up.
  • NightSleep near Yosemite. This is what makes Monday morning much better.

Highlights

  • Lower-stress Yosemite approach
  • Oakdale / Mariposa reset stop
  • Optional Tunnel View at evening light
  • Overnight near Yosemite
  • Early Monday Valley access

Food

Lunch before leaving Davis • dinner in Mariposa/Oakhurst/El Portal or packed near lodging
🏨 Best lodging zones: El Portal, Yosemite Valley, Oakhurst, Mariposa
Because you leave after 1 PM, do not overpack Sunday. Save the actual Yosemite sightseeing for Monday morning.

Day 4 — Monday, June 15

Yosemite Morning + Davis Return

☀️ Yosemite Valley:
Start early • Avoid midday parking stress
📍 Yosemite lodging → Valley highlights → Davis
🕘 Start 7:00–8:00 AM • Return Davis around evening
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~170–220 miles back to Davis
🗺️ Open Yosemite return route 🎟️ Yosemite fees

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 7:00 AMEnter / move into Yosemite Valley early. The overnight pays off here.
  • 8:00 AMTunnel View or Valley viewpoint. Best photo stop before the day gets busy.
  • 9:00 AMLower Yosemite Fall walk. Easy, scenic, and good for the whole group.
  • 11:00 AMYosemite Village / lunch. Restroom, food, and souvenir stop.
  • 1:00 PMEl Capitan Meadow. Relaxed final Valley stop.
  • 2:30–3:30 PMStart return drive. Stop in Oakdale/Manteca if dinner or coffee is needed.
  • 7:00–8:30 PMArrive Davis. Reset before San Francisco on Tuesday.

Highlights

  • Tunnel View
  • Lower Yosemite Fall
  • Yosemite Village
  • El Capitan Meadow
  • Scenic return drive

Food

Breakfast near lodging • lunch in Yosemite Village • dinner on the drive back
⭐ This is now the main Yosemite sightseeing day

Day 5 — Tuesday, June 16

San Francisco Icons

🌉 San Francisco:
Layers + secure parking
📍 Davis → Alcatraz / Fisherman’s Wharf / Pier 39 / Golden Gate Bridge → Davis
🕘 Leave 7:00–8:00 AM • Return around evening
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~170 miles round trip
🗺️ Open SF route 🎟️ Alcatraz tickets

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 7–8 AMLeave Davis. Aim for one parked location near Pier 33 / Fisherman's Wharf.
  • MorningAlcatraz if tickets are available. If not, use the waterfront + Golden Gate plan.
  • MiddayFisherman's Wharf lunch. Keep the car parked and walk the waterfront.
  • AfternoonPier 39 + Ghirardelli. Casual, flexible, and easy after the Yosemite overnight.
  • Late afternoonGolden Gate viewpoint. Battery Spencer or Vista Point depending on fog and parking.
  • EveningReturn to Davis. Leave no bags or valuables visible in the car.

Highlights

  • Alcatraz if ticketed
  • Fisherman's Wharf
  • Pier 39
  • Ghirardelli / waterfront stroll
  • Golden Gate Bridge viewpoint

Food

Waterfront lunch • snacks at Pier 39 • dinner either in SF or back near Davis
⭐ Tuesday is better than weekend SF for crowds and parking
