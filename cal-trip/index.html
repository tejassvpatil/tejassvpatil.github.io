Day 1 — Friday, June 12

Yosemite Day Trip

☀️ Yosemite Valley:
83°F / 52°F • Sunny
📍 Davis → Yosemite Valley → Davis
🕘 Leave 5:30 AM • Return around 8:30–9:00 PM
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~340 miles round trip
🗺️ Open Yosemite route 🎟️ Yosemite fees

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 5:30 AMLeave Davis. Keep breakfast, water, and snacks packed before departure.
  • 7:30 AMOakdale / Groveland quick stop. Coffee, restroom, top off gas if needed.
  • 10:00 AMEnter Yosemite Valley area. Start with Tunnel View if parking is manageable.
  • 10:30 AMTunnel View + Bridalveil Fall. Short photo stops; do not overpack the first half.
  • 12:15 PMLunch in Yosemite Village. Use this as restroom + reset time.
  • 1:30 PMLower Yosemite Fall walk. Easy scenic walk if everyone has energy.
  • 3:30 PMEl Capitan Meadow. Relaxed final Valley stop before driving out.
  • 4:45 PMStart return drive. Dinner on the way back, likely Oakdale / Manteca side.
  • 8:30–9 PMArrive Davis. Early sleep after the longest day.

Highlights

  • Tunnel View
  • Bridalveil Fall
  • Yosemite Village
  • Lower Yosemite Fall
  • El Capitan Meadow

Food

Quick breakfast packed / Oakdale stop • lunch in Yosemite Village • dinner on return
⭐ Best because Yosemite is coolest on Friday
⛰️ Optional upgrade: stay 1 night near El Portal or Oakhurst if you want a more relaxed pace.
2
Day 2 — Saturday, June 13

San Francisco Icons

🌤️ San Francisco:
71°F / 57°F • Sun + clouds
📍 Davis → Alcatraz / Fisherman’s Wharf / Pier 39 / Golden Gate Bridge → Davis
🕘 Leave 7:00 AM • Return around 9:00 PM
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~170 miles round trip
🗺️ Open SF route 🎟️ Alcatraz tickets

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 7:00 AMLeave Davis. Aim to beat Bay Area traffic and parking stress.
  • 8:45 AMArrive near Pier 33. Park once; do not move the car around the wharf area.
  • 9:30 AMAlcatraz ferry / tour. Morning tour is best before the waterfront gets crowded.
  • 12:15 PMFisherman’s Wharf lunch. Casual lunch and restroom break.
  • 1:30 PMPier 39. Sea lions, shops, quick photos.
  • 3:00 PMGhirardelli / waterfront stroll. Keep this flexible depending on energy.
  • 4:30 PMGolden Gate Bridge viewpoint. Use Battery Spencer or Vista Point depending on fog/parking.
  • 6:00 PMStart return drive. Dinner either before leaving SF or back near Davis.
  • 8:30–9 PMArrive Davis. Keep valuables hidden in SF throughout the day.

Highlights

  • Morning Alcatraz tour
  • Pier 39 sea lions
  • Fisherman’s Wharf lunch
  • Ghirardelli / waterfront stroll
  • Golden Gate Bridge viewpoint

Food

Brunch or early lunch at Fisherman’s Wharf • snacks at Pier 39 • dinner back in Davis
⭐ Mild city weather = perfect SF day
3
Day 3 — Sunday, June 14

North Bay Redwoods + Coast

🌤️ Armstrong / Bodega Bay:
Redwoods + cool coast
📍 Davis → Armstrong Redwoods → Bodega Bay → Point Reyes → Davis
🕘 Leave 7:00–7:30 AM • Return around 8:30 PM
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~270 miles round trip
🗺️ Open Armstrong + Coast route 🌲 Armstrong park info

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 7:00 AMLeave Davis. Bring layers; the redwood grove and coast will feel much cooler than Davis.
  • 9:15–9:30 AMArrive at Armstrong Redwoods. Park, restroom, and begin the main grove walk.
  • 9:30–11:15 AMPioneer Nature Trail / main grove. Easy redwood walk; look for Colonel Armstrong Tree and Parson Jones Tree.
  • 11:15 AMDrive toward Bodega Bay. Scenic Russian River / Sonoma County drive with buffer.
  • 12:30–1:30 PMLunch near Bodega Bay or Guerneville. Keep this flexible based on hunger and parking.
  • 1:45–3:00 PMBodega Bay coast. Coastal views, short walk, coffee/snack, optional seafood/chowder.
  • 3:00–4:15 PMDrive toward Point Reyes Station. Treat Point Reyes as the flexible add-on if the day runs long.
  • 4:15–5:30 PMPoint Reyes Station / one scenic stop. Easy final stop before heading back.
  • 6:00 PMStart return to Davis. Dinner/snack stop on the way if needed.
  • 8:30–9:30 PMArrive Davis. Longer than the Muir Woods version, but more impressive redwoods.

Highlights

  • Armstrong Redwoods main grove
  • Pioneer Nature Trail
  • Colonel Armstrong Tree
  • Bodega Bay coastal views
  • Point Reyes Station

Food

Coffee stop in Santa Rosa/Guerneville • lunch near Bodega Bay • Point Reyes/Sebastopol snack or dinner on return
🌲 Biggest redwood experience on the trip
4
Day 4 — Monday, June 15

Lake Tahoe Escape

☀️ South Lake Tahoe:
82°F / 43°F • Mostly sunny
📍 Davis → Emerald Bay → South Lake Tahoe → Davis
🕘 Leave 7:00 AM • Return around 8:00 PM
🛣️ Approx. driving: ~220 miles round trip
🗺️ Open Tahoe route 🏞️ Emerald Bay info

Suggested stop-by-stop timeline

  • 7:00 AMLeave Davis. Check road/traffic before committing to the mountain drive.
  • 8:15 AMPlacerville breakfast stop. Coffee, restroom, snacks for the lake.
  • 10:30 AMEmerald Bay overlook. Main photo stop; parking may be tight even on weekdays.
  • 12:00 PMSouth Lake Tahoe lunch. Keep it casual near the lake or Heavenly Village.
  • 1:30 PMLakeside walk / beach time. Choose a low-effort lake stop instead of overdriving.
  • 3:30 PMHeavenly Village or scenic drive. Flexible final activity based on energy.
  • 5:00 PMStart return drive. Dinner on the way back if needed.
  • 8:00 PMArrive Davis. Return car prep / fuel plan if needed.

Highlights

  • Emerald Bay overlook
  • Lakeside walk
  • Heavenly Village or beach time
  • Scenic return drive

Food

Breakfast stop in Placerville • lunch by the lake • dinner on the way back
⭐ Weekday Tahoe = lighter traffic
